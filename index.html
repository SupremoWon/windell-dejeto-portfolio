04 / Philosophy

How I approach the work

01
Start with the process, not the dashboard.

A chart is only useful if it points at where a process is actually losing time, quality, or money. I trace the number back to the operation before I trust it.

02
Make the report someone can act on.

KPI reporting only matters if leadership can read it once and know what to do next. I'd rather cut a chart than leave one that just decorates a slide.

03
Bring engineering discipline to data work.

Lean Six Sigma taught me to question variation before I explain it. I carry that same rigor into how I clean, validate, and interpret data.

04
Stay close to the tools that ship work.

Power BI, SQL, Python, and Excel aren't ends in themselves — they're how analysis actually reaches the people who need to make a decision.
